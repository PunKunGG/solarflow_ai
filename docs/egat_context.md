# EGAT context used for the concept

The EGAT Green I 2026 Circular Innovation Challenge asks teams to transform a process or create a new business model using innovation/technology/digital technology to advance EGAT's circular economy, with practical benefit to EGAT. The program includes prototype/POC/pilot development and DVF testing.

EGAT's 2025 sustainability report states that EGAT is increasing renewable generation and lists 17 hydro-floating solar hybrid projects totaling 2,725 MW planned for acceleration by 2030, as well as Mae Moh Solar Farm Phase 1 at 38.5 MW. It also notes grid modernization and renewable-energy forecasting to manage increased renewable penetration and variability.
